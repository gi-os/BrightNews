# News 3.4.1

Category labels on the briefing read `WORLD`, not `WORLD  12 STORIES`. Every category is a
dozen, so the count said the same thing on every line.

opens on a tap. A dozen wire headlines are proof, not reading, and they were pushing the
end-of-story controls a screen and a half down.

No schema change. Installs over 3.4.0 and keeps everything.
