# News 3.4.2

**Kagi is fetched once a day, when the edition lands.** Kagi News publishes around noon UTC —
eight in the morning here — and not again until tomorrow, so a category is now fetched exactly
once per edition: the first time the app is opened or refreshed after today's edition is due.
Every other refresh, pulled or automatic, re-reads your feeds and newsletters and leaves Kagi
alone; there is nothing new there to get. A category you have just followed is fetched at once.

No schema change. Installs over 3.4.1 and keeps everything.
